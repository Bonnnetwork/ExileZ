01 / The server

About ExileZ: ReZurrection

ExileZ: ReZurrection is a community-operated DayZ server for PC players. Our goal is to provide a challenging survival environment and a stable place for the community to play.

Supporting the server is entirely optional. Players can join and enjoy the core server experience without purchasing a package.

02 / Monetization disclosure

Supporter packages

The following is the intended package structure and a description of each proposed perk. Prices are shown as listed package prices; no recurring billing period is represented here.

Supporter tier

VIP

$10
  • Priority queue access when joining the server.

This tier provides a queue convenience only. It does not include an in-game equipment or currency reward.

Enhanced supporter tier

VIP+

$20
  • Priority queue access.
  • Proposed bonus spawn items: one jacket, a compass, and a map.

The proposed spawn items are intended as basic utility and convenience items, not weapons, ammunition, currency, or high-tier equipment.

Important — VIP+ item approval: The jacket, compass, and map are disclosed here as proposed VIP+ benefits and are subject to Bohemia Interactive's monetization rules and any required written approval. These items will not be offered as a purchasable perk unless and until their inclusion is approved. If they are not approved, the package will be revised before being offered.
03 / Where support goes

How contributions are used

Supporter contributions help offset the costs of operating and maintaining ExileZ: ReZurrection, including:

  • Dedicated server hosting and infrastructure.
  • Server maintenance, configuration, and technical administration.
  • Community operations and ongoing upkeep.

Contributions support the continued operation of the community server; they are not required to access the server.

04 / Player fairness

Fair-play commitment

  • Purchases are optional; no purchase is required to play.
  • The core gameplay experience remains available to all players.
  • We do not list weapons, ammunition, vehicles, or in-game currency for sale in these packages.
  • Any proposed in-game reward will only be enabled if it complies with applicable Bohemia Interactive rules and receives any required approval.
Our intention is to keep support focused on helping sustain the server while maintaining a fair and enjoyable environment for the wider player community.
