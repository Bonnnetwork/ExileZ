01 / The server

About ExileZ: ReZurrection

ExileZ: ReZurrection is the return of ExileZ: Syberia. Pioneering the iconic Syberia mod (Now called TerjeMedicine/Skills). We are a community-operated DayZ server for PC players. Our goal is to keep the authentic DayZ Survival experience while adding new and refreshing features that expand the DayZ experience!.

Supporting our server is entirely optional, but deeply appreciated. But more than anything, we are just so glad to have you part of our community!

02 / Monetization disclosure

Supporter packages

The following is intended to provide a clear explanation and structure of our Donation Monetization!

Supporter tier

VIP

$10
  • Priority queue access when joining the server.

This tier provides a queue convenience only.

Supporter+ tier

VIP+

$20
  • Priority queue access.
  • Bonus spawn items: Jacket, Compass, and a Map.

VIP+ is intended to provide our supporters an extra basic utility and convenience. While having a special way to identify themselves. There will never be any item that offers an advantage.

Clan Package

Clan Package

$50
  • One priced package for Clans who want to show their pride!
  • One custom Cosmetic Clan Item of your choice distributed to all members of the clan at spawn.

The proposed spawn item must be a cosmetic item Only! (Most notable examples: Poncho, Armbands, Hoods/hats, Jewelry, etc). Base Items such as a unique Flag or Poster can be done on request after making a ticket! Items such as weapons, ammunition, currency, or high-tier equipment are prohibited. Items that have an existing function such as Helmets, Vests, Clothing, or Weapons. Can be considered but will NOT be given at spawn. These will be found and looted in the world!

Important: Special requests depend on the work and time it takes.
03 / Where support goes

How contributions are used

Supporter contributions help offset the costs of operating and maintaining ExileZ: ReZurrection, including:

  • Dedicated server hosting and infrastructure.
  • Server maintenance, configuration, and technical administration.
  • Community operations and ongoing upkeep.

Contributions support the continued operation of the community server! We the developers thank you for your consideration but more importantly being here to experience us!

04 / Player Equality

Fair-play commitment

  • Purchases are always optional; no purchase is required to play the server itself.
  • The core gameplay experience remains available to all players.
  • We do not list weapons, ammunition, vehicles, or in-game currency for sale.
  • All proposed in-game rewards will only be enabled if it complies with applicable Bohemia Interactive rules and receives any required approval or adjustments if necessary.
Our intention is to keep monetary support focused on helping sustain the server rent, and development. While maintaining a fair and enjoyable environment for the wider player community.
